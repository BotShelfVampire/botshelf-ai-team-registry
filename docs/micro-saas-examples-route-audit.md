## Current price and requirements

The official submission page currently presents three paid tiers:

- **Basic — $3:** permanent directory listing, review within seven days, and a permanent backlink from the submitted landing page.
- **Featured — $29:** no backlink required and one do-follow link.
- **Sponsored — $147:** permanent listing, review within 24 hours, featured placement for 30 days, two newsletter mentions, and one do-follow link.

The official terms say submission does not guarantee publication. Every paid submission is reviewed by hand; products that are not a fit are declined and refunded. Paid placement terms apply only after checkout is completed.

No free listing tier is shown on the current official submission page. All three routes are excluded under the no-spend rule. The Basic tier is also excluded because it requires an unapproved permanent backlink on the BSV landing page.
