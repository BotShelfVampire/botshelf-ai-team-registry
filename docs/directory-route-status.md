# Directory Route Status

Updated: 2026-09-28 (Asia/Tokyo)

This index consolidates the public route audits and prepared submission packages for BotShelf Vampire. It is an operational de-duplication and accuracy aid. It does **not** record or imply that a route has been submitted, accepted, scheduled, or published.

## Product-surface rule

- **Marketplace:** ready-to-use AI Team product offers.
- **Build Library:** implementation and evidence material organized by job, runtime, verification state, human approval boundary, visible failures, known limitations, and artifacts.
- A directory entry must name one primary subject. The other surface may appear only as a clearly separate supporting reference.

## Status legend

- **Prepared / not submitted:** public copy is ready, but no external form, account action, or submission has been completed.
- **Audited / not submitted:** official route and terms were reviewed; no external submission was made.
- **Paid-only / not submitted:** no usable $0 route is currently available.
- **Blocked:** a required fee, unavailable slot, account-only fact, terms restriction, duplicate-history gap, or other guard prevents safe submission.

## Submission guard

Before any future directory action:

1. Re-open the exact official route and confirm it is active.
2. Check the public directory and authorized account history for listings, drafts, pending reviews, rejections, or final failures.
3. Confirm the selected route still costs exactly $0 and does not require a trial, badge, backlink, reservation, contract, checkout, or negotiation.
4. Confirm the proposed subject is either the Marketplace or the Build Library and that the other surface remains visibly separate.
5. Use an approved contact identity only at the final authorized step.
6. Preserve the confirmation page, receipt, message ID, or live URL.
7. Record submitted, delivered, replied, accepted, published, declined, paid-only, undeliverable, and no-response as separate states.

## Counting rule

The public audits, packages, and this index are completed, externally verifiable repository actions. Research, prepared copy, blocked access, account-gated checks, and unsubmitted forms do not count as a directory submission, acceptance, or publication. A live listing is counted only after its public URL can be independently verified.
