## Account and submission controls

The official posting guide says that:

- A personal account is required; a company account cannot post a product.
- A newly created account must complete onboarding before it can post.
- A Hunter is optional; the maker can post the product directly.
- The flow collects the product URL, name, tagline, topics, pricing status, thumbnail, gallery, description, maker identity, optional social account, first comment, and launch timing.
- A draft can be created without choosing a date.
- A scheduled launch can be set within 30 days and goes live at 12:01 AM Pacific time.

These requirements make the final draft, maker identity, and schedule owner-controlled actions. No account, onboarding, OAuth, draft, schedule, or submission was created.

## Reversibility and duplicate risk

Product Hunt's official removal guidance says a submitted product rarely disappears automatically and there is no self-service dashboard deletion. Removal requires Product Hunt support.

The official relaunch guidance says:

- Makers should generally wait at least six months between posts for the same product or company.
- Products sharing the same root domain must observe the six-month gap.
- A relaunch requires a significant update; minor UI or pricing changes are not enough.

Official sources:

- Removal: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/484941-can-you-remove-my-post
- Relaunches and domain gap: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/484934-can-i-relaunch-my-product
