A direct public-page check in the cloud browser reached the site's robot/security challenge. No attempt was made to solve or bypass it.

Accordingly:

- no account was created;
- no login was attempted;
- no username, email, password, product data, founder data, link, image, or launch date was entered;
- no form was submitted;
- no message was sent;
- no promotion was selected;
- no payment, reservation, negotiation, or checkout was attempted.

Any future submission must be completed manually by the owner in a normal browser session, only after the live form explicitly confirms a $0 route and authenticated history shows no existing draft or prior submission.
