## Route verified

Startup Buffer's current public FAQ states that a startup can be submitted for review without paying. The official three-step form asks for:

1. startup name;
2. website URL;
3. business email, used to send a private edit link;
4. a one-sentence short pitch;
5. a full description of at least three to four sentences;
6. a clear product or homepage screenshot, maximum 500 KB;
7. optional country, city, primary category, up to eight tags, YouTube URL, LinkedIn page, X profile, and Facebook page.

The current support FAQ says reviews can take one to two months due to demand.

No form data, business email, screenshot, social profile, contact message, or submission was sent during this audit.
