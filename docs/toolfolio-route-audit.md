## Editorial fit

Toolfolio accepts functional, maintained, stable, current products with clear value, credible presentation and legal/ethical integrity. Acceptance is discretionary and not guaranteed.

The guidelines also exclude direct competitors, including tool directories or marketplaces focused on the same niche. That creates an editorial-fit risk if the ready-to-use BotShelf Vampire Marketplace is submitted.

If a future $0 route becomes available, the proposed subject is the **BotShelf Vampire Build Library**, under Agents or Coding:

> Implementation and evidence records for AI teams, including runtime, verification state, human approval boundaries, visible failed evaluations and known limitations.

The Marketplace remains a separate product surface and must not be presented as the same listing.

## Refund inconsistency

The paid page contains conflicting statements:

- near the pricing section, Toolfolio says rejected tools will receive a 100% refund;
- the FAQ says all listing payments are final and non-refundable.

This unresolved conflict is an additional reason not to enter checkout or purchase a listing.
